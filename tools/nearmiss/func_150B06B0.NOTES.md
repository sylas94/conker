### The generalisable lever: PAIRWISE LINE JOINS under `-g3`

This is the piece worth taking to other functions. With `-g3` IDO attaches a line number to
every instruction and its list scheduler groups by them, so **putting two adjacent statements
on one source line merges two scheduling groups** and can be worth 10 points a time.

The trap that hid it here for two waves: the old note had tested joining the WHOLE tail onto
one line (much worse, 51 rows) and this wave had tested SPLITTING each statement across two
lines (all 24 exactly inert). Both are the wrong granularity. It is the **single adjacent
pair** that pays. Sweep it as a greedy climb — each round joins every remaining adjacent pair
and keeps the best:

| round | candidates | best |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 22 | 570 (`unk30`+`unk32`; `unk3A`+`unk44` and `unk44`+`unk46` tie at 570) |
| 2 | 21 | 560 (`unk3A`+`unk44` on top) |
| 3 | 20 | no improvement — stop |

Not every function responds: the same climb on the sibling `func_1512B730` moved nothing at
all (25 join candidates, every one byte-identical). Cheap to test, so test it.

## Verdict — BAIL, with the reason stated plainly

The instruction multiset is identical and the residual is a pure list-scheduler priority tie on
`addiu s0` (dest) vs `addiu t4` (src). Ten independent spellings of the copy produce byte-
identical output; the one construct that would reorder the operands (a memcpy call) is not
inlined by IDO; 6,244 permuter iterations under exact frame and stack-offset gates never
improved on a −5 cosmetic edit; and the complete pairwise-swap and move neighbourhoods of the
only block involved bottom out at 574. There is no source handle left that this wave can find.

What IS parked is the 560 form: two line joins and one chained assignment, all of which a human
plausibly writes and none of which changes a token. A field-order SCRAMBLE reaches **558**
(recorded in the sweep tables above) and is deliberately NOT parked: it buys a couple of points
of stack-offset alignment noise by writing the struct fields out of numeric order, which makes
the C less like something a human wrote while leaving all nine reordering penalties in place.

If someone revisits this, revisit it with a NEW IDEA about operand ordering, not with more
search — the neighbourhoods named here (all transpositions, all pairwise swaps, all moves, all
