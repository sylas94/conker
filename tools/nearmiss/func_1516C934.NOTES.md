### A QUANTITATIVE MODEL FOR THE s2/s4 INVERSION, AND WHAT IT SAYS IS MISSING

Golden `s2=arg1, s4=&buf`; mine `s2=&buf, s4=arg1`. Counting web references and weighting the
main-loop body by `W`:

```
        out-of-loop refs   in-loop refs      weighted
golden  arg1  6            4                 6 + 4W
        buf   1            5                 1 + 5W
mine    arg1  6            3   (<- one fewer: the un-duplicated loop-bottom lw)
        buf   1            5
```
`arg1 > buf` in golden requires `W < 5`; `arg1 < buf` in mine requires `W > 2.5`. **Both hold for
`W = 3` or `4`, so the model is consistent and the margin is exactly one in-loop reference.**
`&buf`'s five in-loop references are all irreducible (`addu v0,s4,s0` for `&buf[len]`, `move a0,s4`
for `func_150428D4`, `move a3,s4` ×3 for the three `func_150417AC` calls), so the only lever is
**+1 in-loop `arg1` reference** — i.e. missing instruction #2 itself. Chicken-and-egg confirmed
quantitatively, not just narratively.

### Why `p` occupies the 8th local slot and `flags` does not

The frame decode (below) forces **exactly eight** 4-byte locals between `buf` and `w`. Seven are
forced by the codegen: `cond, i, len, first, dx, y, xoff`. The eighth was `flags` and is now `p`:

* `cond` is **required**: removing it and writing the overflow test as one bare `if` scores

`p` passes the load-bearing test: deleting it costs an instruction and 113 points (1836 @198).
