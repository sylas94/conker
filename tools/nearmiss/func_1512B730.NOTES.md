## The levers that made this function (unchanged, still true)

* **`u8` kind locals.** Caching the four latch bytes as `u8 k0..k3` instead of `s32` took the
  diff from opcode-diff 36 (238 insns) to opcode-diff 3 (247 insns) in one edit. With `s32`
  IDO CSEs the literal `2` into one t-register; with `u8` every comparison emits the
  immediate form, which the assembler expands to a per-site `li at,2` — which is what golden
  has. *Generalisable: when golden materialises the SAME small constant repeatedly in `$at`
  and your output hoists it into one t-register, narrow the compared variable's declared type.*
* **One load has to be issued before the two float loads.** The opening block's six
  independent loads: exactly 10 of the 720 orderings score the optimum, and they are exactly
  the ones with `k0`'s load first. The rest spread from +25 to +6800.
* Cache the four latch bytes in locals but write the record address as an EXPRESSION (the
  `REC` macro). Golden reloads `arg0->unk23D` after every `sb` to the record but never
  reloads the latch bytes.
* `((struct S *)D_800DC0C0)[arg0->unk23D]` (direct subscript) gives golden's base-first
  `addu t1,v0,t6` and keeps the 0x4C multiply as `multu <idx>,v1`; a byte-offset form expands
  it into a 5-instruction shift chain.
* Both arms of `if (arg0->unk23C != 0) … else …` compute the identical expression. That is an
  **original-game redundancy**, not a transcription error; merging them loses 6 instructions
  and must not be "fixed".
* `arg2` is a real third parameter that is never read; it is homed (`sw a2,0x8(sp)`) purely
  because IDO reuses `a2` for the 0/1 return flag. No `arg2 = arg2;` idiom is needed.
